## Filtered results

- **Productivity and Remote Work — U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics** — https://www.bls.gov/productivity/articles-and-research/remote-work/productivity-and-remote-work.htm — Quantitative remote-work prevalence, but only indirect mention of productivity.
- **Productivity and Remote Work — U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics** — https://www.bls.gov/productivity/notices/2024/productivity-and-remote-work.htm — Points to other resources without quantitative evidence itself.
- **2020 Year-End Report and Remote Jobs Statistics** — https://www.virtualvocations.com/blog/annual-statistical-remote-work-reports/2020-year-end-report-and-remote-jobs-statistics/ — No relevance assessment was returned.
- **State of Remote Work 2020 — Owl Labs** — https://resources.owllabs.com/state-of-remote-work/2020 — Survey data on happiness and stress, but no qualitative/mixed-methods analysis of satisfaction.
- **STATE REMOTE — Owl Labs** — https://resources.owllabs.com/hubfs/website/sorw/2020/owl-labs_sorw-2020_report-download_FINAL_07oct2020.pdf — Quantitative corporate report; no qualitative or mixed-methods findings.
- **Exploring the Dynamics and Implications of Remote Work during and post-COVID-19: A Qualitative Analysis** — https://www.researchgate.net/publication/375230442_Exploring_the_Dynamics_and_Implications_of_Remote_Work_during_and_post-COVID-19_A_Qualitative_Analysis — Topic is remote-work dynamics, but the title/snippet did not mention satisfaction.
- **Remote working and employee engagement: A qualitative study of British workers during the pandemic** — https://www.researchgate.net/publication/352519680_Remote_working_and_employee_engagement_A_qualitative_study_of_British_workers_during_the_pandemic — Focuses on engagement rather than job satisfaction.
- **Analyzing the Long-Term Impact of Remote Work on Organizational Efficiency and Employee Performance** — https://www.researchgate.net/publication/385074263_ANALYZING_THE_LONG-TERM_IMPACT_OF_REMOTE_WORK_ON_ORGANIZATIONAL_EFFICIENCY_AND_EMPLOYEE_PERFORMANCE_EXPLORING_KEY_TECHNOLOGIES_AND_MANAGEMENT_PRACTICES — Limited detail on collaboration or team dynamics.
- **2021 Remote Work Report** — https://cdn.buttercms.com/nfmrsT4Sl2MekyB4Ny1S — Survey methodology without specific collaboration findings in the snippet.
- **State of Remote Work 2020 — Owl Labs & Global Workplace Analytics** — https://globalworkplaceanalytics.com/downloads/state-of-remote-work-2020 — Single-year survey, no indication of repeated panel waves.
- **DatanomyUK Remote Work Survey Sample Dataset — GitHub** — https://github.com/DatanomyUK/RemoteWork-Survey-Sample-Dataset---PBI — One-off sample dataset; no evidence of panel design.
- **RemoteWork-Survey-Dashboard — GitHub** — https://github.com/Akhil-V-Nair/RemoteWork-Survey-Dashboard — Two survey dates, but unclear whether the same respondents were followed.
- **Remote Work, Wages, and Hours Worked in the United States** — https://www.bls.gov/osmr/research-papers/2023/pdf/ec230050.pdf — Focuses on wages/hours, not the three outcomes or a dedicated remote-worker panel.
- **Remote Work, Wages, and Hours Worked in the United States** — https://www.bls.gov/osmr/research-papers/2023/ec230050.htm — Same wages/hours analysis as the PDF, not a panel for the target outcomes.
- **The impact of COVID-19 on employee productivity and the** — https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0351968 — Discusses perceptions of remote-work productivity but did not identify a panel dataset.
- **Post-COVID remote working and its impact on people productivity and the planet** — https://www.researchgate.net/publication/371139363_Post-COVID_remote_working_and_its_impact_on_people_productivity_and_the_planet_An_exploratory_scoping_review — Scoping review, but the snippet did not establish a focus on panel tracking.
- **The Impact of Remote Work on Employee Productivity and…** — https://ijrti.org/papers/IJRTI2507097.pdf — Focused on productivity during COVID-19, not a multi-outcome panel study.
